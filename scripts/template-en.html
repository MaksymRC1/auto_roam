Anyone who has ever planned a long road trip across Europe knows the pain: navigation apps are not enough. You have to manually calculate mileage, check fuel prices in different countries, look for overnight stays, and figure out toll roads.

We built AutoRoam (autoroam.com.ua) — a free web app that automates all of this in seconds.

🚗 What makes AutoRoam special:

  • Smart Fuel Calculator: Enter your car's consumption and get the exact cost in your currency based on real-time prices;
  • Smart Stops: Suggests the best cities for overnight stops after long hours of driving to prevent driver fatigue;
  • Vignettes in 1 Click: Identifies where toll roads begin and provides direct official links for payment, avoiding fines and reseller fees;
  • No App Downloads: Works directly in the browser as a PWA on smartphones, tablets, or laptops.
